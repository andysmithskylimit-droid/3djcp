# JCP Lawn Service design direction

Preserve the continuous live Three.js garden through the first four chapters. Natural materials, smaller individual leaves, dense narrow grass, restrained wind and grounded mower motion are the direction. Do not replace the scene with photo parallax or confine it to a separate widget.

Reserve clear zones for type: creamy white hero type on deep green; dark green service text on a warm cream panel; dark solid play controls. Keep the garden bright when it moves left beside services. Lower business sections use warm cream and muted sage with clear 14–16px body copy. Headlines use Manrope; body text uses DM Sans.

Retain services, process, attributed reviews, FAQs, detailed lead form and contact/map. Avoid unsupported claims about history, prices, guarantees or customer quotes. The directory rating must remain attributed and dated. Enquiries prepare visitor-sent email drafts.

Motion uses one rendering loop and camera ownership: GSAP/Lenis scrolling, draggable orbit and tilt, wind shaders, per-blade cutting, natural acceleration/turning, and interpolated lighting. Honor reduced motion, pause/reset, mobile geometry limits, and stop rendering while solid lower sections cover the scene.
