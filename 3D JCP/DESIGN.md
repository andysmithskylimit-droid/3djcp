# JCP Lawn Service

Direction: a continuous full-screen living 3D landscape. Real scene interaction drives the entire experience. Photographic imagery is reserved for WebGL fallback.

Palette: forest #173d2b, leaf #375c3c, pale sage #e8ede3, daylight #f6f7f2, chartreuse flower #d6e6a2, ink #20352a.
Type: Manrope for generous, tightly set display headlines and navigation; DM Sans for comfortable body copy. Left-aligned content, with large rhythmic changes in scale.
Layout: full-screen interactive 3D opening → orbiting service exploration → playable mowing scene → landscape promise → contact and collapsible map. The garden persists through the first four chapters with interpolated camera poses.

Hero: [brand | navigation | estimate] / [large headline + CTA over shaded lawn] / [location | scroll cue].
Service: [intro] / [expandable service choices | large photo with contextual caption].
Signature: [headline + care controls | freely inspectable miniature lawn].

Review: removed generic ratings, invented project portfolio, neon-on-black palette and redundant numbered cards. The 3D scene illustrates care, rather than claiming real project outcomes. Service categories are estimate interests and availability is confirmed directly. Motion follows user intent, with one cinematic hero entrance and restrained scroll depth. No forced intro or scrolling obstruction.
