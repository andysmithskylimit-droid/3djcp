# JCP Lawn Service — natural interactive 3D

## Preview
Run `node preview.cjs`, then open http://127.0.0.1:4173. Use HTTP for JavaScript modules. Static hosting uses the contents of `dist`. This revision has not been published.

## What changed
A continuous Three.js garden connects the hero, service explorer, mowing playground, and lifestyle CTA. Individual curved leaves flutter in adjustable wind. Dense grass bends in traveling gusts and is cut blade by blade. Scanned bark textures, HDR environment lighting, stone grain, a thinner soil cutaway, mower hardware and fabric details replace the simpler treatment. Mower acceleration, turning, wheel travel and slight vibration are coordinated. Drag in both directions to orbit and tilt; reset, daylight/dusk, breeze, and ambient pause controls remain available.

High-contrast copy uses a quiet hero zone, a cream service panel, and dark playground controls. Detailed services, getting-started guidance, sourced review information, six FAQs, an expanded enquiry form, contact information and the map follow on solid editorial surfaces. Reduced motion and lower mobile geometry counts are supported. Rendering pauses behind lower page content.

## Enquiries and reviews
Both forms include service, frequency, yard condition and preferred timing. Visitors review a draft and send it through their own email app to juancpantoja78@gmail.com. There is no submission backend, automatic delivery, storage or booking. Scope, price and availability require direct confirmation.

The reviews section attributes a public directory snapshot: JCP Lawn Service, 4.8 from 4 reviews, matched by address and telephone on October 14, 2026. Source: https://www.whosmypro.com/landscaping/north-highlands-ca/ . This is not a live Google rating. No individual testimonial quotes were verified or invented. Google Maps links provide access to the current public profile.

## Assets and dependencies
Bundled Three.js 0.170.0, GSAP 3.13.0, ScrollTrigger, Lenis 1.3.11. Procedural garden imagery illustrates the experience and does not depict an actual JCP project. Generated garden image is a WebGL fallback.

CC0 assets from Poly Haven: Spruit Sunrise HDR by Greg Zaal (https://polyhaven.com/a/spruit_sunrise), Bark Brown 01 by Rob Tuytel (https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01). Leafy Grass textures are retained but unused. Google Fonts and Maps need an internet connection.
